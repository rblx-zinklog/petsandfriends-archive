Welcome to the Archive

A quiet corner of the internet where stories, essays, guides and poems are kept for anyone who cares to read them.

The Pets & Friends Archive is a personal library of texts and publications written by one author on a variety of subjects: the animals we share our lives with, the friends we make along the way, and whatever else seems worth setting down on paper. Everything here is original, and everything is free to read.

New writings are added from time to time. You may browse the complete catalogue, search for a particular subject with the search page, or begin with the most recent publications below.
