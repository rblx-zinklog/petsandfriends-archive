Welcome to the Archive

The official archive of all pets and friends alike.

The Pets & Friends Archive is a public library of documents and publications written by multiple authors on a variety of subjects: pets, friends, historical events, and other information adjacent to the Pets & Friends Facility. Everything here is real, and everything is free to read.

New writings are added from time to time. You may browse the complete catalogue, search for a particular subject with the search page, or begin with the most recent publications below.
