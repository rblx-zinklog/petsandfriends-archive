About the Archive

The official archive of documents managed by the Pets & Friends Facility.

What is this place?

The Pets & Friends Archive is an online collection of texts and publications managed by the Pets & Friends Facility. Subjects range from pets, friends, significant figures, major events and more to be released in the future. At the moment the archive holds … publications, the most recent added on ….

How it is organised

Each publication is filed under a single category, and may carry a few tags to help you find related pieces. The catalogue lists everything and can be filtered by category or year; the search page looks through titles, descriptions, categories and tags.
