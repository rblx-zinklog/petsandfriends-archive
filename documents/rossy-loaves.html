Rossy Loaves is the visionary founder and sole proprietor of the Pets & Friends Facility, an organisation dedicated to the protection, care, and security of pets and friends alike.

Established under his leadership, the facility reflects Rossy's unwavering commitment to providing a safe and welcoming environment for all pets and friends. As its sole founder, he oversees the organisation's direction, values, and operations, ensuring that its founding mission remains at the heart of everything it does.

Known for his dedication, ambition, and strong sense of responsibility, Rossy Loaves stands as the central figure behind the Pets & Friends Facility and its continued development.

His Upbringing

Loaves was born a male on April 26, 1978, into a wealthy family in the seaside city of Nice, France. Following his birth, his family hosted a cultural ceremony where they sacrified multiple sheep. This ceremony ultimately led the family into severe legal trouble and backlash from the local farmers, in which they responded by relocating northwest to Voiron. Loaves spent most of his childhood in Voiron before moving out at the age of 19.

  • Father: Victor Loaves
  • Mother: Jeanette Loaves
  • Brothers: Jacques Loaves, Raffaele Loaves (missing)

Early Adulthood

Following his departure from Voiron in 1997, Loaves relocated to Paris, where he initially pursued a career in private enterprise. Having inherited a considerable sum of money from his family, he was under little financial pressure to seek conventional employment. Instead, he spent much of his early adulthood travelling throughout Europe, attending private exhibitions, scientific conferences, and various gatherings of wealthy collectors.

It was during this period that Loaves developed an increasingly unusual interest in biological abnormalities, particularly organisms that appeared to demonstrate characteristics inconsistent with established scientific understanding. What began as a passing curiosity gradually developed into a defining personal interest. He became fascinated by the possibility that undiscovered forms of life existed beyond the reach of conventional zoological institutions.

Loaves reportedly maintained an extensive private collection of unusual biological specimens, documents, and research materials. Although the precise origins of many of these materials remain unclear, they would eventually form the foundation of his later work.

The Discovery of Abnormal Creatures

In the early 2000s, Loaves began dedicating an increasingly significant portion of his personal wealth towards locating and acquiring unusual creatures. Through private collectors, independent researchers, and various undocumented sources, he encountered reports of organisms commonly referred to as "Pets and Friends".

Unlike ordinary animals, these creatures were believed to possess highly unusual physical characteristics, behavioural patterns, and, in certain cases, abilities that could not readily be explained through conventional biological research. Some were described as remarkably intelligent, others as possessing extraordinary physical properties, while several appeared to exhibit entirely unfamiliar biological structures.

Loaves became particularly concerned with the lack of suitable institutions capable of housing these organisms. Existing animal facilities were generally unequipped to accommodate their unusual requirements, while scientific institutions were often more interested in studying their abnormalities than providing for their long-term welfare.

He consequently began developing an ambitious proposal: an independently funded institution that would combine secure containment, residential care, and scientific research under a single organisation.

The Formation of His Private Research Operation

By 2005, Loaves had begun assembling a small network of trusted individuals to assist with his growing collection. Initially operating from privately leased properties, the operation focused on the acquisition, observation, and temporary housing of abnormal creatures.

His considerable personal fortune allowed him to finance specialised accommodation, research equipment, security infrastructure, and the employment of a limited number of assistants. Unlike traditional zoological establishments, Loaves insisted that every creature under his supervision receive individual consideration, with accommodation and care arrangements adapted to its particular characteristics.

Nevertheless, the operation quickly outgrew its original premises. The increasing number of creatures, combined with the need for more sophisticated research facilities and security arrangements, made it clear that a permanent establishment would be necessary.

During this period, Loaves reportedly became increasingly secretive regarding the precise location and nature of his activities. This was attributed to concerns surrounding the safety of the creatures, unwanted public attention, and the possibility of outside interference.

The Founding of the Pets & Friends Facility

In 2009, following several years of preparation, Rossy Loaves formally established the Pets & Friends Facility. Privately financed through his personal wealth and independent investments, the organisation was conceived as a permanent institution dedicated to the housing, protection, observation, and scientific investigation of abnormal creatures.

The establishment represented the culmination of Loaves' ambitions throughout the preceding decade. Rather than operating as a conventional laboratory or animal sanctuary, the facility was designed to incorporate elements of both, providing a controlled environment in which unusual organisms could be accommodated while remaining accessible to qualified researchers.

Loaves assumed the position of sole founder, proprietor, and principal financial benefactor. He retained complete ownership of the organisation, allowing him to establish its internal policies and long-term objectives without the influence of external shareholders or institutional investors.

The facility's founding philosophy was centred around three principal objectives:

  • Protection: To provide secure accommodation and appropriate care for abnormal creatures that could not safely or suitably exist within conventional environments.
  • Research: To investigate the biological, behavioural, and environmental characteristics of Pets and Friends, expanding the available understanding of these unusual organisms.
  • Preservation: To maintain and protect abnormal species, ensuring that individual specimens and potentially rare varieties remained under responsible supervision.

Expansion and Institutional Development

Following its establishment, the Pets & Friends Facility underwent a period of considerable expansion. Loaves directed substantial financial resources towards the development of specialised accommodation, observation rooms, medical facilities, administrative departments, and dedicated research laboratories.

As the organisation grew, its operations became increasingly structured. New personnel were recruited to oversee creature welfare, conduct scientific investigations, maintain facility infrastructure, and manage the security of the premises. Despite this expansion, Loaves remained closely involved in the institution's major decisions and continued to exercise authority over its overall direction.

His approach to management was notably personal. He considered the facility to be an extension of his original vision rather than an ordinary commercial enterprise. Consequently, major acquisitions, research initiatives, and institutional developments were generally subject to his approval.

The organisation also began accepting abnormal creatures from external sources, including private individuals and independent collectors who were unable to provide suitable long-term accommodation. This gradually transformed the facility from a privately maintained collection into a much larger residential and scientific institution.

Legacy and Continuing Influence

Today, Rossy Loaves remains the central figure behind the Pets & Friends Facility. His decision to dedicate a substantial portion of his personal fortune towards the establishment of an independent institution fundamentally shaped the organisation's identity and purpose.

Although the facility has developed considerably beyond its original operations, its founding objectives remain closely associated with Loaves' original ambitions. The institution continues to house, protect, and research abnormal creatures, maintaining its position as an independently operated establishment under his ownership.

Whether regarded as an ambitious entrepreneur, an unconventional philanthropist, or an obsessive researcher, Rossy Loaves has established an institution unlike any other. His life's work remains inseparable from the Pets & Friends Facility, an organisation that began as a personal fascination and ultimately developed into his defining achievement.
