A poem of an unknown author, found in northern Penland in 2003.


[Verse 1]
Oh my Pen,
You magic camel.
You found me in my den,
Starving in my flannel.

[Verse 2]
I owe it to you,
My whole bloodline.
Took me in and made me stew,
Then ran off to the alpine.

[Verse 3]
You'll never know your worth,
On this green Earth.
But thank you,
You were my breakthrough.
