For a hound who has earned his rest

The afternoon has folded up its light and laid it on the rug beside your head. You chase a rabbit through the edge of night, though all the fields you know are warm instead.
Your paws go running through some distant lane; a whisper tells me you have caught the wind. Then all is still, and then it starts again, the long, slow music of a day well spent.
So sleep, old friend, and let the clock run down. The door is locked, the kettle's nearly cold. There is no better kingdom in the town than one small dog, and what his dreams can hold.
